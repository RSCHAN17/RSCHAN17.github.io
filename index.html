What is Gig Bingo?

My name is Raza, I've always had a love for live music, so far been to over 50 gigs (over 20 this year). Gigs and concerts are a lot of fun, but the more you go to, the more you notice some clichés, and why not turn those clichés into a game? Hence the birth of Gig Bingo!
